# M15 voice samples — for the owner's native Uzbek spot-check

Generated by `make m15-key-smoke` on 2026-08-16. **These are not content.** They exist so a
native Uzbek speaker can judge the two Azure voices before any driver hears them — E3
§E3.4.2 requires that spot-check, and it is a person rather than a key.

| file | voice | what it says |
|---|---|---|
| `officer_en.mp3` | `en-US-GuyNeural` | "Good afternoon. Where are you headed today?" |
| `coach_uz_sardor.mp3` | `uz-UZ-SardorNeural` | the coach explaining, in Uzbek, that the officer asked where you are going |
| `coach_uz_madina.mp3` | `uz-UZ-MadinaNeural` | the same line, other voice |

**The question for the reviewer** is not "is it understandable" — it is whether a driver
would trust it: does the Uzbek sound like a person explaining something, or like a machine
reading? A coach voice that sounds wrong teaches a driver to stop listening, and the
teaching loop is the whole product.

**Not stored in F6, deliberately.** Content audio is permanent-class and belongs to a
released phrase variant. Storing spot-check output as content is how an unreviewed voice
ends up attached to a driver-facing line.

**No Uzbek content is synthesized at scale until the owner confirms these.**
